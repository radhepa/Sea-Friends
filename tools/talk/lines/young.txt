## young.chat
Can you look at two things at once? Really at once? I tried, with one eye, and it just went sideways.
I am practising the stillness. You sit by the jelly pond and you do not move and you do not blink, and if you are still enough a glowjelly drifts against you. None has yet. I think they can tell when I am thinking about it.
My spines are still soft. When I come of count they will set hard, like the grown ones'. Then I will look very fierce. I practise fierce in the pond.
I have a toy guardian. It is made of prismarine and it has a little eye. It does not bite. Real ones bite. I am not allowed to go and see one.
Do your kind have counts? When I first felt the pull in my spines, I was given my number. I will not tell you it. You have to be a very good friend first.
What is the sun like? Is it a big glowjelly? Does it sting?
The grown ones will not tell us about the Silence. I asked an Archivist, and their spines went up so fast that I said sorry before they answered.
How do you breathe air? Where does it go? Does it just sit inside you?
I want to be an Eyeguard. Or a Lightwright. Or an Archivist. Or the one who looks after the jellies. I have decided to be all of them, one after another, slowly.
The merfolk fry came down to the court once, and they were so fast! They swam round the spire twice before I finished saying hello.
Is it true that on land things fall down instead of floating? Everything? Even you? Even lamps?
I counted all the lamps in {place}. Then I counted them again and got a different number. The Archivists say this is why we write things down.
When I am old I will know the whole Long Tide by heart. I already know the beginning: "I see you, Two-Eyes. Hear the Long Tide as the Oldest have always told it." That part is easy.
I watched a glowjelly split in two once. The whole thing! Nobody believed me. Then an Archivist wrote it down, so now it is true.
Why do you blink so much? Are your eyes very dry? Do they get tired of looking?
I am going to see the old halls one day. Not inside. Just from far away. The Eyeguards say maybe, when I am older. They say it very slowly, which means no.
Is it true you only have one name? We do too! But we have a court as well. You can borrow ours. "{player} of {place}." See? It fits.
I tried to stay awake as long as an Eyeguard. I fell asleep sitting up. Everyone said that counted as the stillness. I do not think it did.

## young.past
In the Long Tide we lived in the great halls, and they were full of light and song. That is the beginning of the Long Tide. I know it by heart. The middle part I am still learning.
The halls had names like the Hall of Spines and the Deep Gate. I like the Hall of Spines best. It sounds like my crest when I am cross.
We used to have guardians as watch-beasts. Real ones. I asked if we could have one again, and everyone went very quiet.
I asked what the Silence was. Everyone said "later". It has been later for a very long time.
There was an Oldest called Ossiveth, who led everybody out of the halls and only looked back once. I asked what Ossiveth saw. That is another "later".

## young.night
I am not supposed to be up. I am practising the stillness. In bed. It is very hard to tell from sleeping.
I like the evenings best, because the Long Tide gets told. The night is just the part after, when I am supposed to remember it.
The Eyeguards watch all night. I asked if they get scared. They said no. Their spines said a little bit.
When I cannot sleep I count the lamps through the window. I always fall asleep before I finish. One day I will finish.

## young.work
Watching the glowjellies. It is very important. If nobody watches them, they might do something and nobody would know.
I am watching the jellies for the Spongekeeper. They drift left, then right, then left. I am remembering it all down.
Practising the stillness. You are not supposed to talk while you practise. So I am not practising right now. I am explaining.
I am helping! I carried a lamp to the Pact crate. It was heavy. I did not drop it. Nearly, but not.
I am learning the Reckoning of Tides. One tide, a rising and a falling. Two tides, a day. Twenty-eight, a turning. Thirteen turnings... wait. A Long Count! I knew it.
I am feeling for the pull. You can feel it in your spines if you are very still. Can you feel it? In your... what do you have? Elbows?
The jelly by the far wall has not moved for a whole tide. It is either asleep or better at the stillness than me. Probably both.
I am counting sea pickles. Some have four lights and some have one. I like the ones with one. They are trying hard.

## young.heart
Sometimes I worry I will never hold the stillness long enough. Then I worry that worrying is moving. Then I give up and watch the jellies.
I want to make the grown ones proud. Mostly I make them sigh. A sigh is a kind of proud, I think. A slow kind.
When my spines go up I feel ashamed, because everyone can see. Do your kind have anything that shows how you feel? You go red, someone said. Is that worse?
I am glad you came. Not many people come here. I do not mind the quiet. I just like it better with you in it.
I do not want to stop counting, ever. I told the Oldest Eye. The Oldest blinked slowly at me and said: good, then.

## young.adventure
Will you tell me what is out past the lamps? I am not allowed. You are. That is not fair, but tell me anyway.
If you go to the old halls, will you look at them for me? Only look. And then tell me what colour the light is.
The Eyeguards say the Unseeing have two eyes like you but see nothing. I think that is sad. I would rather have one and see everything.
When you go out in the deep, keep a lamp with you. That is a rule. I made it up, but it is a good one.
If you meet a guardian, do not stare at it. That is what everybody tells me. I think it is because guardians are better at staring than us, and it is embarrassing.
Bring me back something from the land. Anything! I have never held anything that has been in the sun, except a breath-stone, and that does not count.

## young.joke
What do you call a glowjelly that will not glow? A jelly. That is my best joke. I made it.
Why did the sponge go to the Archive? To soak up knowledge. The Archivist did not laugh. Their spines did, a little.
I tried to wink like you do. I just closed my eye. Then I could not see anything and I swam into a lamp.
How many eyes does a Two-Eyes have? Two! That is why they are called that! I worked it out myself.
Knock knock. Oh. We do not have doors. That is the end of the joke. I think it is funnier that way.
If the Eyeguards watch the court, who watches the Eyeguards? Me! I watch them all day. They hate it.
What is a guardian's favourite game? Staring. They always win. That is not a joke, really. It is true.
