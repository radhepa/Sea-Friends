# Fry are born with pale silver tails that "blush" their family colour on Blushing Day.
# Canon: see merfolk.txt (the Barnacle King is their bogey; Old Pell; Old Coil; "Big Eyes" = the Prismari).

## child.chat
Do you have toes? How many? Can they wiggle on their own? Do they have names?
The Barnacle King lives in Lowmere and he eats fry who don't eat their kelp. I eat all my kelp. Mostly.
When my tail blushes, I want it to be violet. Gran says it'll be teal like the family. I'm going to blush violet anyway, on purpose.
I found a seadragon in the garden and named it Sir Fluffington. It isn't fluffy. That's the joke.
I'm not allowed past the arches. I went past the arches once. Only a little. Don't tell the wardens.
What's rain? A bottle from my cousin said rain. Is it the sea falling down? Why would the sea fall down?
I can do a loop! Watch! ...I'll do it better next time.
Old Pell said a whale once swallowed a whole town and the town just lived in there. I'm going to live in a whale.
If you knock on coral three times, nothing bad happens. I knocked a hundred times. Nothing bad will happen for ages.
Nautili are grumpy. I'm grumpy sometimes. That's why we're friends.
When I grow up I want to be a warden. Or a singer. Or a seadragon.
Did you know seadragon dads carry the eggs? On their tails! I'm going to tell everyone. I've told everyone.
My friend says if you whistle over the drop-off, the deep whistles back. I didn't try. I almost tried.
I've got the best shell collection in {place}. Forty shells. Forty-one with the broken one.
What's walking like? Is it like swimming but you fall over?
I learned three verses of the Song today! Then I forgot two. One's still in there, though.
Do sandtoes have Blushing Day? What colour did your feet go?
I'm going to find Old Coil and ride her, and everyone will be so jealous.
We built a fort out of sponge cushions. It's the best fort ever. Grown-ups aren't allowed. You can come, though.
Why do you have two legs? Wouldn't one tail be easier? I'd have picked the tail.
I can make bubble rings! Look! ...That one was a bubble square. That's harder.

## child.work
My job is feeding the seadragons. They eat tiny shrimps. I don't eat the shrimps. Mostly.
I'm chasing seadragons! It's very important work. They get bored if nobody chases them.
I polish the shrine steps. Well, I'm supposed to. Mostly I slide down them.
I help count the sea pickles on the paths. There's loads. I stopped at a hundred and something.
I carry kelp bundles to the racks. They're bigger than me. I'm very strong.
I'm learning songs at the shrine. The Elder makes a funny face when I sing it wrong. I sing it wrong on purpose sometimes.
I'm practising tag. That's work. Gran says it's not work. Gran is wrong.
I sort shells for the divers. Good ones in the basket, broken ones on the path, pretty ones in my cushion.
When I'm big I'll have a real job. Right now my job is asking questions. I'm very good at it.
I'm helping the wardens! I watch the arches and shout if I see a drowned. I've shouted loads. None were drowned. One was a squid.

## child.heart
Sometimes I'm scared of the dark past the lamps. But then I look at the Heart and it's glowing, so it's all right.
I like you because you listen. Grown-ups pretend to listen. You actually do.
I miss my cousin on land. I've never met them. I still miss them. Is that silly?
When I'm big I want to be brave like the wardens and kind like the singers. Both at once.
If you're ever sad, you can borrow my best shell. Only borrow. Then you give it back. But you can borrow it.

## child.joke

Knock knock! Who's there? Kelp! Kelp who? Kelp me, I'm stuck in a joke!
What do you call a nautilus that won't move? A nauti-lazy! I made that one up.
Why don't clams share? Because they're shellfish! Everyone knows that one. I still like it.
What did the sea say to the beach? Nothing! It just waved! Because waves! The sea waves!

What's the Barnacle King scared of? Me! That's not a joke. That's a promise.

## child.past
Gran says before the Flood everyone had feet like you. Even Gran's gran's gran. I don't believe it.
Wenna went into the dark with one lantern and sang until the Prismari came. I'm going to do that. With two lanterns.
Old Coil carried the first fry down from the Flood on her shell. A dozen at once! I'd have sat at the front.
We heard the Flood story today. The water came in and never went out. I held my tail the whole time.
Lowmere used to be a town like ours. Now the drowned live there. I'm going to fix it when I'm big.

## child.neighbors

We call them Big Eyes. Not to their faces! They're very tall and very polite and very spiky.
On Lantern Night a Prismari came up and sang so low it made my tummy buzz. Best night ever.
